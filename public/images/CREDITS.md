# Earth imagery

`earth-texture.jpg`: NASA Blue Marble Earth texture, distributed with the three.js examples.
Source: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/examples/textures/planets/earth_atmos_2048.jpg

`earth-fallback.jpg`: Blue Marble 2002. Credit: NASA's Earth Observatory.
Source: https://science.nasa.gov/resource/blue-marble-2002/

The rotating globe is a visual illustration, not live Earth data. The static NASA image remains available when motion is reduced or WebGL cannot run.
